AIRBNB ARRIVAL READY

Open index.html in a browser. Keep the assets folder beside it.

Or visit:
https://dilatedtime.github.io/airbnb-arrival-ready/

To review the solution, open Trips, then Cedar House. The Arrival plan tab leads to the saved pack, companion copy and entry recovery.

New labels show the proposed additions. Use Show added features to hide the labels. What's new explains each change.

Test conditions lets you try low battery, no signal, a broken lock and a dead phone. You can reset and repeat the journey as often as you want.

The account menu opens the host and support screens. The full source is at:
https://github.com/dilatedtime/airbnb-arrival-ready

All property details, people, reviews, prices and bookings are fictional examples. Calls, sharing and reservations are simulated. No real booking or payment is made.
